## Privacy Policy

**Cipher Notes** is designed with absolute privacy in mind.

* **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry.
* **No Network Access:** The app does not request or possess the `INTERNET` permission. Your data never leaves your device.
* **Local Encryption:** All your notes are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256 (GCM). You are the sole owner of your data and encryption keys.
